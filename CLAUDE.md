These are standing rules for how Akash ("Akki") Puri wants to work on this project. Follow them every session without being asked again.

## How to work with Akki

- **Go slow, step-by-step.** Confirm before moving to the next step. Don't combine multiple actions or jump ahead.
- **Explain changes in plain English before making them.** Akki wants to understand *why*, not just see it done.
- **Always ask before committing or pushing to GitHub.**
- **Show results/preview before anything goes live.**
